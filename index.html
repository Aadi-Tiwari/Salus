Know what ran.

Reports survive a server restart. Agent runs record runtime events, exit status, source hashes, and patch validation. No simulated activity or automatic success claims.

Keep the scope clear.

These are passive checks of a page and its same-origin scripts. Salus does not authenticate, crawl an entire application, or certify it as secure.
